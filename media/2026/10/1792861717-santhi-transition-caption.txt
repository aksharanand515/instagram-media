Save this for the day your Marichyasana feels steady. Then try flowing straight into Flying Lizard.

How I move through this arm balance transition:

1. Release the bind slowly and plant both hands on the mat.
2. Bring the bent knee high onto the back of the same arm, close to the shoulder.
3. Shift your weight forward until your feet feel light.
4. Lift, then reach the back leg long.

The lean forward is the whole secret. Take it slowly, and keep a cushion under your face for the first few tries.
