Steady and at ease. Patanjali asks for both in every posture.

स्थिरसुखमासनम् · sthira sukham āsanam (Yoga Sūtra II.46)

Handstand, Eka Pada Koundinyasana, Sirsasana, a wide-legged forward fold and Hanumanasana, practised in our Fort Kochi shala. The strong poses ask for steadiness. The deep ones ask for ease. The practice asks for both at the same time, which is why the breath stays slow even upside down.

Classical Hatha yoga in Fort Kochi, Kerala, every day at 7:30 am and 3:30 pm. All levels, taught in English.
