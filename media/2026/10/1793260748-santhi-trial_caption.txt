You don't lift into Flying Lizard with your arms. You lean until your feet float.

From Marichyasana, this is the order I teach it in:

1. Bind. Settle the twist and breathe.
2. Release. Unwind slowly and plant both hands.
3. Lean. Shoulders travel forward past your wrists.
4. Float. The feet get light on their own.
5. Extend. Reach the back leg long.

Most people try to push up into an arm balance. Lean further instead, and keep a cushion under your face while you find it.
