Which one would you try? Comment 1 for Eight-Angle pose or 2 for Firefly pose.

1. Ashtavakrasana, Eight-Angle pose. Hook one leg over the arm, cross the ankles, lean forward and stretch both legs out to the side. The toughest part is getting the legs through.

2. Titibhasana, Firefly pose. Shoulders behind the knees, hands under the heels, then sit the hips down slowly until the feet lift. Toes up, chest open, look up.

Two separate arm balances, both built over time with steady practice. Filmed in class in Fort Kochi, Kerala.
