Dragonfly pose (Parsva Bhuja Dandasana) is an arm balance where most of the work is placing the foot and the arm in the right spot.

1. Standing, take the foot into half lotus with the sole facing out, placed over your quad.
2. Sit low and twist the spine.
3. Bring the tricep under the middle of the sole.
4. Lean forward until the legs float, then stretch them long to the side.

If you fall, open the knees like a pigeon and lean again. I tried this every single day for a month or two before it came. Even today I fall all the time.
